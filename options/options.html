Blocklist

One entry per line. Plain words match whole words, ignoring case. Use /pattern/i for a regular expression, and start a line with // for a note. Changes save automatically.

The blocklist is paused. Your entries are kept, but nothing is hidden until you turn it back on.

Tip: on YouTube, open the ⋮ menu on any video or comment and choose Block this channel or Block this video.

Opening a blocked video pauses it behind a notice. Opening a blocked channel's page takes you back to the YouTube home page.
